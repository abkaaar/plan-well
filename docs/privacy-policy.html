Privacy Policy

Plan Well · Effective date: 29 September 2026 · Last updated: 29 October 2026

Plan Well (“the App”) is an offline-first personal planner for tasks and captured opportunities. This policy explains how the App handles information.

No data collection

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with Plan Well. There is no account system, no analytics SDK, and no advertising identifier tracking by the App.

No internet access by the App

The App does not declare the INTERNET permission for its own networking. Plan Well does not contact Plan Well servers. Core planning features work without a network connection.

Data stored on your device

Tasks, categories, reminders, opportunities, linked tasks, and attachment metadata you create are stored only in a local database on your device (SQLite via Android Room). Opportunity image/file attachments are stored in the App’s private files directory. Reminder schedules use the device’s AlarmManager / WorkManager. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it (for example, the optional JSON export to Downloads).

On-device text recognition (OCR)

Optional opportunity extraction uses on-device ML Kit text recognition. Images and recognized text are processed locally to suggest unconfirmed candidates (such as titles, URLs, or dates). Candidates are never treated as confirmed facts until you review them. Plan Well does not upload your images or OCR text to Plan Well servers.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to show reminder alerts you configure.
  • Exact alarms (Android 12+) — to fire reminders at the times you set.
  • Boot completed — to restore scheduled reminders after a device reboot.
  • Photos / media (when you choose) — only if you pick an image from the gallery to attach or run OCR.

These permissions are used only for local reminder and capture functionality.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect data from children. It is suitable for a general audience and does not include social features or ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
